## 3. Dynamic Grid Alignment Logic (The "Drift Fix")

This is the most critical implementation added to `answer_detector.py`. 

### The Problem
When the OMR is printed or scanned, the paper physically stretches or shrinks by small amounts (e.g., a 0.4 pixel shrink per row). By row 32, this accumulates to a 12+ pixel drift, causing the scanner to look completely off the bubble and return `UNCERTAIN | 0.01`.

### The Solution (Applied to `student-report`)
Before scanning any section, the detector now runs `calculate_dynamic_alignment(thresh_image, x_coords, y_coords)`:
1. **Column Projection:** Takes a vertical slice of the thresholded image at the column's X-coordinates and sums the pixels horizontally to create a 1D signal.
2. **1D Cross-Correlation:** Tests multiple theoretical scales (from 96% to 104% stretch) and vertical shifts (from -12 to +12 pixels).
3. **Dynamic Scaling:** Finds the scale and shift that maximizes correlation with the actual printed bubbles.
4. **Application:** Dynamically recalculates all `y_coords` for that section based on the true physical stretch of the page.

**Result:** The ROI bubbles now perfectly snap to the center of the physical bubbles, entirely eliminating vertical drift regardless of printer quality or camera angle.
