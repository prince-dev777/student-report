1. HARDWARE SETUP & SCANNER CABLE EXTENSION
================================================================================
Problem: 
  The Shreyans Desktop 2D QR Scanner comes with a standard ~1.5 meter USB cable,
  which is too short to reach from the reception laptop/desk to the entrance table.

Solution:
  Buy a "USB 2.0 Male-to-Female Extension Cable" (Amazon / Local Electronics Shop):
  - Recommended Length: 3 Meters or 5 Meters (Price: ~Rs. 150 to Rs. 250)
  - Recommended Spec: USB 2.0 / 3.0 Extension Cable (Braided, Gold Plated)
  - Search Term on Amazon: "USB 2.0 Extension Cable Male to Female 3M / 5M"

Physical Placement at Coaching Entrance:
  1. Place the Shreyans Tabletop QR Scanner on a clean acrylic stand or table at
     the student entrance gate at chest/eye level (approx 3.5 to 4 feet height).
  2. Run the extended USB cable along the wall/floor using cable clips/casing
     directly into the Reception Laptop USB port.
  3. Keep the scanner tilted slightly upwards (15-20 degrees) so students can
     swipe their laminated ID Card or phone QR without bending down.

2. LAPTOP & COACHING OPERATING SCHEDULE (12 HOURS DAILY)
================================================================================
Laptop Power & Display Settings:
  1. Keep the Reception Laptop plugged into the AC Charger at all times.
  2. Windows Power Settings:
     - Open Windows Settings -> System -> Power & Battery -> Screen & Sleep.
     - "When plugged in, turn off my screen after": Set to "5 minutes" (saves display).
     - "When plugged in, put my device to sleep": Set to "NEVER" (CRITICAL!).
     * Note: Turning screen off saves electricity and laptop screen life, while
       CPU, Node.js Server, and Scanner keep running in the background.

Daily Morning Startup (7:30 AM):
  1. Turn on the Laptop and open "Career Xone" desktop application.
  2. Go to "Attendance" -> Tab 4: "⚡ Live QR Scanner Kiosk Mode".
  3. Ensure the cursor/focus is inside the Kiosk scan box.
  4. In "SMS Center", ensure WhatsApp status is "Connected & Ready".
  5. The system is now live! Every student scan takes 0.05 seconds.

4. QR ATTENDANCE KIOSK & WHATSAPP ALERT FLOW
================================================================================
How Attendance & WhatsApp Work in Real-Time:
  1. Student shows QR ID card to Shreyans Desktop Scanner.
  2. Desktop app receives Roll Number (e.g. 8451) in 0.05 seconds.
  3. System automatically calculates:
     - Dynamic Session (Morning Batch / Evening Batch based on current time).
     - Check-In (Entry) vs Check-Out (Exit) based on previous punches today.
  4. App plays a pleasant audio chime ("Attendance Marked: Prince Sharma").
  5. Backend sends a personalized WhatsApp message to the parent phone:
     "Dear Mukesh Sharma, your ward Prince Sharma (Roll: 8451) has safely 
      ENTERED Career Xone Institute at 08:15 AM."
  6. Record is saved locally and synced instantly to MongoDB Atlas Cloud.

5. OMR SCANNER EVALUATION FLOW
================================================================================
How Test Papers & OMR Sheets are Processed:
  1. Conduct NEET/JEE 100/180/200 Question Exam using Master OMR Sheets.
  2. Feed scanned OMR JPG/PNG images into Career Xone OMR Tab.
  3. Python OMR Engine (V2 - Locked & Calibrated) reads bubbles in 0.2s/sheet.
  4. Automatically calculates: Total Marks, Negative Marking, Subject-Wise Marks,
     AIR Rank, Batch Rank, and Percentile.
  5. 1-Click WhatsApp Scorecard dispatch sends detailed digital scorecards to parents.
  6. Parents can also view full OMR answer bubble overlay directly on Parents Portal!
